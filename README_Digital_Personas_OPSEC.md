# Digital Personas, OSINT & OPSEC

**Ethical Synthetic Identity Research and Operational Security**  
**Author:** Pedro Gonçalves

## Overview
This portfolio project explores synthetic digital personas for authorized OSINT exercises, privacy testing and cybersecurity education. It reviews profile-generation tools, compartmentalized workspaces, private communications, browser fingerprinting and operational-security practices.

## Focus areas
- Synthetic profile data and AI-generated portraits
- Privacy-oriented email and messaging services
- Browser isolation, fingerprinting and WebRTC/IP leak awareness
- VPN, Tor and security-focused operating systems
- Compartmentalization, credential hygiene and ethical constraints

## Documented outcome
The study compares tools and proposes an OPSEC workflow. A dedicated verification phone number was not obtained, so some persona-registration steps were **not completed**. The project does not claim verified anonymity or a fully deployed persona.
